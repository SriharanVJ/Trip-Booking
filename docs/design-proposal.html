3 · Buttons

Travels' 12 gold variants collapse into 7. Navy = default action · Gold = brand CTA ("Book now", "Get started") · Gradient = hero moments only.

Radius 12px (rounded-lg) · heights 36/44/48 · focus ring 2px gold · travels' built-in loading spinner is kept and shared.

4 · Forms & validation — one pattern everywhere

Travels' floating-label Input is retired in favor of one top-label field (clearer, and consistent with the driver app's data density). Required fields carry a gold asterisk; errors are red 12.5px under the field; focus is always the gold ring.
